Ooty


Ooty is a beautiful hill station in Tamil Nadu, India. It is famous for its cool climate, green tea gardens, beautiful mountains, and scenic views.

Top 5 Attractions

  1. Ooty Lake - Enjoy boating and beautiful scenery.
  2. Botanical Gardens - Explore a variety of plants and flowers.
  3. Doddabetta Peak - Enjoy amazing mountain views.
  4. Nilgiri Mountain Railway - Experience a scenic train journey.
  5. Tea Gardens - Visit green tea plantations.

Must-Try Foods

  • Ooty Varkey - A famous local baked snack.
  • Homemade Chocolates - A popular Ooty speciality.
  • Fresh Tea - Enjoy locally grown Nilgiri tea.
  • South Indian Dosa - A traditional local meal.
  • Hot Soup - Perfect for the cool weather.
